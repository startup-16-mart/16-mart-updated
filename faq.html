Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, direct WhatsApp ordering process, payment options, and islandwide delivery.

Ordering via WhatsApp

How can I place an order with SIXTEEN MART?

Ordering is simple, fast, and secure. Simply browse our website, click on any product card to open the Quick View pop-up, choose your desired quantity (1 to 5 units), and click "Order Now via WhatsApp". This generates a pre-formatted message with your chosen item and quantity, and opens WhatsApp directly so our team can immediately confirm your delivery address.

Why do you use WhatsApp instead of a standard checkout cart?

As a new growing lifestyle brand, WhatsApp ordering allows us to provide warm, personalized human customer service with zero checkout friction or complex account logins. You can ask questions about item specifications, confirm stock in real time, and receive live delivery tracking directly on your phone.

Can I order multiple different products in one message?

Yes, absolutely! When you click Order on any product, you can simply type any additional items you want into the WhatsApp chat before pressing send, or message us directly with your custom product list.

Why is quantity capped at 5 units per order?

Because our curated lifestyle products are imported in carefully controlled batches with limited stock, we cap quantities at 5 units per order to ensure fair availability for all customers. If you are interested in wholesale or bulk corporate gifts, please mention it to us in the chat!

Shipping & Islandwide Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM are dispatched on the same or next business day. Colombo and suburban areas typically arrive within 1 to 2 business days. Outstation and provincial deliveries typically take 2 to 4 business days via our trusted courier partners.

Do you deliver islandwide?

Yes! We deliver door-to-door to all districts and locations across the country. Our courier will call your provided contact number before arrival to coordinate drop-off.

How much is the delivery charge?

Standard delivery is typically Rs. 350 – Rs. 450 depending on your delivery location and package weight. We frequently offer free delivery promotions on orders over Rs. 5,000 — ask our WhatsApp agent for ongoing specials!

Payment Options

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
1. Cash on Delivery (COD) — Pay comfortably when your package arrives at your doorstep.
2. Bank Transfer / Online Banking — Direct bank deposits or mobile banking transfers prior to dispatch.

Do I need a credit card to place an order?

No credit card is required! With Cash on Delivery and direct bank transfers, you can shop effortlessly without entering card details online.

Product Quality & Warranties

Are your products genuine and checked before shipping?

Yes. Each product in our catalog undergoes rigorous multi-point inspection by our 16 founding team members before packaging to verify motor functionality, charging cables, seal integrity, and aesthetic finish.

What if my product arrives damaged or defective?

We take customer satisfaction seriously. If an item arrives defective or damaged during transit, notify us on WhatsApp with a short video or photo within 48 hours of receiving the package. We will promptly arrange a free replacement or exchange.

Still have questions?

Our team is always online on WhatsApp to answer any specific questions about our catalog, stock, or delivery.
