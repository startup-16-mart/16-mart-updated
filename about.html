Brand Story

The 16 Members & 16 Products Concept

The name SIXTEEN MART is built directly upon our core business concept: offering 16 carefully selected lifestyle products, each curated to represent the 16 founding members of our team.

We are an online lifestyle store created for Sri Lankan consumers, particularly university students and young professionals seeking affordable, functional, and trendy essentials. Our focused selection prioritizes genuine utility, honest pricing, and direct social ordering through WhatsApp.
