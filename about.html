Brand Overview

The 16 Products & 16 Members Concept

The name 16 Mart is based on the business concept of offering 16 selected products. The 16 products represent the 16 members of the group, making the name an essential part of our business identity.

16 Mart is an online lifestyle store that offers affordable, functional, and trendy products for young Sri Lankan consumers. The store focuses on a limited selection of 16 products, including home accessories and beauty accessories. Customers discover the products through digital channels and shop easily through our direct social channels.
